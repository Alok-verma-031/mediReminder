💊 MediReminder

About This Project

🔹 Problem

Many people forget to take their medicines on time, especially elderly patients and busy individuals.

🔹 Solution

MediReminder helps users schedule their medicines and reminds them at the correct time.

🔹 Features

  • Add medicine with time
  • View daily schedule
  • Reminder alerts

🔹 Impact

This system improves health by ensuring users never miss their medication.
